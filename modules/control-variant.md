# Control and Variant Module

Defines setup guidelines for groups.

## Rules

1. **A/B Tests**:
   - Require structured control group and variant group settings.
   - Example: Control (generic outreach) vs Variant (pain-point customized message).
2. **Single Cohort / Channel Tests**:
   - Where a control group is not feasible (e.g. testing a brand new partnership channel), the design compares results directly against the pre-established historical baseline.
3. **Equivalence Scanning**:
   - Ensure target audience segments for control and variant are matched statically (e.g. similar sizes, employee bands, and geography).
